Questions

Good to know

What exactly do I get?

A personal wedding website built from the template you choose, filled with your names, events, venues, photos and song, hosted by us at a link you can share on WhatsApp. Delivered within 2 working days of receiving your details.

Do I need a laptop, an account or any design skills?

No. You fill a short form on your phone. We do the publishing and hosting.

Can I change details after I have shared the link?

Yes. Send us the change and the same link updates. This includes unlimited text and photo changes until your wedding day.

How long does my invite stay online?

Hosting is included until 12 months after your wedding date. Ask us if you need it longer.

Can I use my own song?

Yes, send an MP3 you have the right to use. The music never autoplays; guests tap to play it.

Can I get a custom domain like ourwedding.in?

Not yet. Your invite gets a clean link on our domain. Contact us if a custom domain matters to you.

Is payment secure?

Yes. Checkout is Direct WhatsApp Confirmation and accepts UPI, RuPay, Visa, Mastercard and net banking. We never see your card details.

What if I am not happy?

See our refund policy. If we cannot deliver your invite, you get a full refund.

Do you have templates for other ceremonies?

Sikh, South Indian, Muslim, Christian and Save the date designs are coming soon. Tap “Notify me” on any of them.
