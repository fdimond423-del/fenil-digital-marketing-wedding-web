A Journey Begins

Third and fourth in a queue on College Street

Ananya was third in a queue on College Street for a reprint nobody else seemed to want. Rudra was fourth, and spent the next forty minutes explaining why the earlier edition was the better one.

She bought the last copy. He has not forgiven her, which is more or less how the six years since have gone: Kolkata, then two years of Ananya in Guwahati, then Kolkata again.

Three days, both houses, and the fish will be worth the trip.

With love, Ananya & Rudra
