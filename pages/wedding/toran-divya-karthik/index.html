A Journey Begins

Two horoscopes and a spreadsheet with forty-one tabs

Our families met in March, the horoscopes were read in April, and by May there was a shared spreadsheet. One of the tabs was called Coffee, and it was not a joke.

Karthik was in Bengaluru then and Divya was finishing a thesis in Chennai, so most of it happened on the overnight bus and on the phone afterwards. The muhurtham was the only thing nobody argued about. The priest said 10:15 and that was the end of it.

Come for all four. The coffee tab is still being maintained.

With love, Divya & Karthik
