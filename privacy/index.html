Privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026.

Summary

We collect only what we need to build and host your invite, and to take payment. We do not sell your data. We use the Meta Pixel to measure our advertising (see “What this site loads”). We do not use any other analytics or tracking.

What we collect

  • When you order: your name, phone, email and the wedding details, photos and music you send us to build your invite.
  • When you pay: Razorpay processes your payment. We receive the payment status, amount, order ID and the contact details you enter in Razorpay’s checkout. We never see your full card or UPI credentials.
  • When you message us on WhatsApp: your number and messages, under WhatsApp’s own privacy policy.
  • Server logs: our host (Vercel) keeps standard technical logs such as IP address and request time for security and reliability.

What this site loads

The order form on a template page is not stored or sent anywhere; it only prefills checkout or your WhatsApp message. When you press Buy, your browser loads Razorpay’s checkout script from checkout.razorpay.com. Fonts and images are served from this site.

Meta Pixel

This site uses the Meta Pixel from Meta Platforms. It loads a script from connect.facebook.net and sets cookies so we can measure which ads lead to visits and orders and show relevant ads. It records page views, which template you view, when you start checkout, when you tap WhatsApp, and completed purchases (template and amount). It does not receive your card or UPI details, and we do not send it the order form fields. Meta processes this data under its own privacy policy. You can limit ad tracking in your Facebook or Instagram ad settings, or block it with your browser or an ad blocker; the site works the same either way.

Your invite is public to anyone with the link

Guests can see whatever you put on your invite. Ask us to remove details or take the invite down at any time.

How long we keep data

We keep invite content while your invite is hosted and delete it within 90 days after hosting ends, unless you ask earlier. We keep payment records as required by Indian tax law.

Who is responsible

Fenil Digital Marketing is a brand of Hearth and Kin Hospitality LLP, which is responsible for your data on this site.
