How it began

Our story

  1. Monsoon 2020

    A borrowed book

    A Lucknow library, one copy of a Ghalib anthology and two people who both refused to wait for the other to return it.

  2. Winter 2023

    Letters, not texts

    Aditya moved to Pune for work. Navya wrote him a letter every Sunday, and he kept every envelope.

  3. Diwali 2025

    The question

    A sealed envelope on her desk, a ring inside it and one line in his handwriting. She opened it twice to be sure.
