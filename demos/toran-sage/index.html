A Journey Begins

It started with a wrong number

Rohan was trying to reach a plumber in Jaipur in the summer of 2022. Aarti was in Udaipur, had never owned a wrench, and for reasons she still cannot explain, did not hang up.

Four years, one stubborn long distance, and roughly nine hundred voice notes later, he asked on the steps at Ambrai while the lake went orange behind them. She said yes before he finished the sentence.

We would love you there for all of it. Come hungry.
