How to choose an invitation template

  1. 1Start with the occasion: wedding, birthday, party, baby shower, graduation, anniversary or a company event.
  2. 2Pick the mood: elegant, playful, traditional or minimal. Colors, fonts and photos can all be changed later.
  3. 3Check the layout fits your details: a long programme needs more room than a dinner invite.
  4. 4Preview it on your phone, because most guests will open it there.

Free and premium invitation templates

Every account can use the free templates and send them with RSVPs on the free plan. Premium templates add more designer layouts for when the occasion calls for something special. Use the price filter above to see only the free ones.

Digital invitation templates that do more than a card

A printed card or a picture tells guests the date. A digital invitation template here becomes a live page: the venue on a map, a countdown, the schedule, a reply button, and a personal link for each guest so you know who opened it and who is coming.

Are the invitation templates free?

Yes. Many templates are free to use and send on the free plan. Premium designs are marked and come with the paid plans.

Can I edit a template completely?

Yes. Change the wording, photos, colors and fonts, add your venue and schedule, and preview it before you send it.

How do guests receive the invitation?

You send a link by WhatsApp, email or SMS. It opens in any browser, and guests reply without creating an account.

Can I print the invitation as well?

The invitations are made for sharing online, with RSVPs built in. If you also want a printed card, keep the same design and wording so both feel like one set.
