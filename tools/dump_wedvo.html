Good to know

Do I pay before making my invitation?

No. You can make it and see it with a watermark for free. The watermark goes when you pay.

Can I change it after payment?

Yes. You can edit it for 3 days after payment using your private edit link.

Can guests reply to my invitation?

Yes. After payment, guests can send RSVP replies and you see them on your private replies link.

How long does my link stay live?

Until 30 days after your last function. Unpaid previews are removed 7 days after the last save.
