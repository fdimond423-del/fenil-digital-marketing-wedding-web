What’s in Rangmahal

  • Palace doors that open in 3D as guests scroll, with drifting marigold and rose petals
  • Ganesha blessing with an editable mantra and family invitation
  • Up to four events (Mehendi, Haldi, Sangeet, Wedding by default), each with its own colour, painting, date, time, venue, dress code and Google Maps route
  • Swipeable gallery of your pre-wedding photos
  • The couple’s photo in a painted marble-and-gold frame, with short bios and parents’ names
  • One-tap WhatsApp RSVP with your prefilled message
  • Good-to-know list: hashtag, weather, stay, parking and dress code
  • Instagram link for your wedding handle
  • Live countdown that switches to “Just married” after the wedding
  • Tap-to-play music (your MP3, or our original theme)
  • Large, elder-friendly text; respects “reduce motion” settings

What you get

  • Your invite built from Rangmahal with your names, events, venues, photos and song
  • Hosting on our link until 12 months after your wedding date
  • Unlimited text and photo changes until your wedding day
  • Delivery within 2 working days of receiving your details
  • A short link-sharing message you can forward on WhatsApp

What we need from you

Names and families, event dates, times and venues, 1 couple photo and up to 5 gallery photos, an optional song (MP3 you have rights to), and your WhatsApp number for RSVPs.
