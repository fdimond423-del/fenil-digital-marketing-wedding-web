What’s in Pichola

  • A marigold-garlanded boat that glides across a painted sunrise lake, with its reflection and wake
  • Your names, dates and city rising in the morning sky
  • One event card for the wedding dates, with a Google Maps link
  • Add to calendar button (works on iPhone and Android)
  • The couple’s photo in an arched frame, with short bios and parents’ names
  • Swipeable gallery of up to 3 photos
  • One-tap WhatsApp reply so guests can confirm they’ve saved the date
  • Short planning notes: weather, stay and getting there
  • Live countdown to the wedding day
  • Tap-to-play music (your MP3, or our original theme)
  • Respects “reduce motion” settings

What you get

  • Your invite built from Pichola with your names, events, venues, photos and song
  • Hosting on our link until 12 months after your wedding date
  • Unlimited text and photo changes until your wedding day
  • Delivery within 2 working days of receiving your details
  • A short link-sharing message you can forward on WhatsApp

What we need from you

Names and families, your wedding dates and city, 1 couple photo and up to 3 gallery photos, an optional song (MP3 you have rights to), and your WhatsApp number for replies.
