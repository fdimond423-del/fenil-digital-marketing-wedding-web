Marathi wedding invitation card wording for a Maharashtrian lagna patrika

A Maharashtrian lagna patrika has a gentle, formal rhythm all its own. Here is how a Marathi wedding card is structured, with sample wording you can adapt and the words you will see on almost every card.

Maharashtrian weddings are known for their warmth and their punctuality: the akshata are showered at the exact muhurta, and the card tells guests precisely when to arrive. A Marathi wedding invitation card, or lagna patrika, reflects this with clear timings, respectful language and a closing that invites the whole family.

Please note: the Marathi samples below use simple, standard wording. Family traditions differ between regions such as Pune, Kolhapur, Konkan and Vidarbha, so ask an elder or a fluent Marathi speaker to review your final text, especially honorifics and the muhurta.

The structure of a Marathi lagna patrika

  1. Invocation: "॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥", often with the family deity (कुलदैवत) named.
  2. Hosts and the couple: the parents' names, the bride or groom of the family and the partner with their parents.
  3. The muhurta: the auspicious time of the wedding, written precisely.
  4. The request: asking guests to attend with family and bless the couple.
  5. Other functions: साखरपुडा, हळद, सीमांत पूजन, स्वागत समारंभ.
  6. Closing: आपले नम्र or निमंत्रक followed by the family names, and sometimes a line from the children.

Function lines in Marathi

Marathi cards often use Devanagari numerals (१, २, ३). If many of your guests are more comfortable with 1, 2, 3, it is perfectly fine to use those instead. Whatever you choose, check each weekday against the date for your wedding year.

The children's line

Like the bal manuhar on Hindi cards, many Marathi patrikas end with a sweet line from the children of the family.

Design ideas for a Maharashtrian wedding card

  • Paithani borders in peacock green, purple and gold.
  • Ganesha and kalash motifs, often in a simple line style.
  • Mundavalya, the strings of pearls tied across the couple's foreheads, as a decorative element.
  • Warli-style illustrations for a modern, earthy look.
  • Ashtavinayak or Pandharpur references for families who wish to include them.

Keep the muhurta prominent. At a Marathi wedding, guests plan their arrival around it.

Printed patrika or digital invitation?

Many Maharashtrian families still take printed patrikas to relatives' homes, which is a lovely tradition. For colleagues, friends and out-of-town guests, a digital invitation saves time. Frequently asked questions

What is a Marathi wedding card called?

It is usually called a लग्नपत्रिका (lagna patrika), meaning wedding letter or invitation.

What does चि. सौ. कां. mean before the bride's name?

It stands for चिरंजीव सौभाग्यकांक्षिणी, a traditional blessing meaning a long-lived young woman who wishes for a happy married life. It is used before the bride's name until the wedding.

How do you write the muhurta on a Marathi invitation?

Write the day, date and exact time, for example "शुभमुहूर्त दुपारी १२ वाजून ३५ मिनिटे". Confirm it with your family priest before printing.

Can a Marathi wedding invitation be in English?

Yes. Many families use Marathi for the blessing and family lines and English for event details, or send a separate English invitation to friends and colleagues.
