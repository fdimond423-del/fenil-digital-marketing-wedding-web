Tamil wedding invitation wording, with samples in Tamil script

A Tamil wedding invitation is built around one moment: the muhurtham. Here is how a Tamil wedding card is structured, with sample wording in Tamil and English that you can adapt.

In Tamil weddings, the muhurtham is the heart of the day. Guests arrive early, the nadaswaram plays, and the thaali is tied at the auspicious time. So a Tamil wedding card puts that time front and centre, along with the names of both families and a warm request to come with your whole family and bless the couple.

Please note: the Tamil samples below use simple, common wording. Families from different regions and communities have their own conventions, so please ask an elder or a fluent Tamil speaker to review your final text and confirm the muhurtham with your family priest.

The structure of a Tamil wedding card

  1. Invocation: "பிள்ளையார் துணை" or "ஸ்ரீ கணேசாய நம:", sometimes with the family deity (குலதெய்வம்).
  2. The date and muhurtham, often with the Tamil month and the time window.
  3. The couple: with honorifics and their parents' names.
  4. The venue: the kalyana mandapam with its address.
  5. The request to attend with family and bless the couple.
  6. Other events: நிச்சயதார்த்தம், மாப்பிள்ளை அழைப்பு, வரவேற்பு.
  7. Closing: the host family's name with a welcoming line.

Function lines

Some families hold the reception the evening before the wedding instead. Whatever your order, make sure each line has the day, date, time and the hall's name.

Wording for friends

Design ideas for a Tamil wedding card

  • Kolam patterns drawn in white on a red-oxide background.
  • Kuthuvilakku (brass lamps) and mango-leaf thoranams.
  • Temple gopurams and Chettinad pillars for a heritage look.
  • Jasmine strings and banana leaves for a fresh, festive card.
  • Kanchipuram silk colours: deep red, mustard, peacock green and gold borders.

Frequently asked questions

How do you write a marriage invitation in Tamil?

Open with a blessing such as "பிள்ளையார் துணை", give the couple's names with honorifics and their parents' names, state the date and muhurtham time, the mandapam and address, and request guests to attend with family and bless the couple.

What is the honorific for the groom and bride on a Tamil card?

திருவளர்செல்வன் is commonly used for the groom and திருநிறைச்செல்வி for the bride. Families sometimes use other forms, so follow your family's custom.

Should the muhurtham time be on the card?

Yes. It is the most important detail on a Tamil wedding card, because guests aim to be present when the thaali is tied.

Can I send a Tamil wedding invitation on WhatsApp?

Yes. Digital invitations are widely used, especially for friends, colleagues and relatives abroad. Many families also hand-deliver printed cards to elders.
