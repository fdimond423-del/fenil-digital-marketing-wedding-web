A marriage invitation card in Hindi feels different from an English one. The language naturally carries respect: guests are asked to "grace" the occasion with their presence, the couple are named with traditional honorifics, and the card often closes with the names of everyone eager to welcome the guests. Below you will find the structure, the common words and several full samples.

A quick note before you copy: Hindi wedding wording varies by region and family. The samples below use simple, standard Hindi. Please have an elder or a fluent speaker check spellings, honorifics and the muhurat before you print or share.

The structure of a Hindi wedding card

  1. मंगलाचरण (invocation): a line such as "॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥" or a short shloka.
  2. निमंत्रक (hosts): usually the parents, sometimes "श्रीमती एवं श्री" followed by the family name.
  3. The couple: the bride or groom of the hosting family first, then the partner with their parents' names.
  4. The occasion: शुभ विवाह, पाणिग्रहण संस्कार or शुभ लग्न.
  5. Programme (कार्यक्रम): each function with date, time and venue.
  6. Closing lines: विनीत, स्वागतोत्सुक or दर्शनाभिलाषी, followed by family names.

The bal manuhar: a sweet tradition

Many Hindi cards end with a short line written as if from the children of the family, called बाल मनुहार. It is playful and guests love it.

Hindi or English or both?

Bilingual cards are now very common. A popular approach is Hindi for the blessing, the parents' line and the closing names, and English for the event details so that every guest, including friends from other states, can read times and venues easily. A digital invite makes this simple because you can show both without crowding a single printed sheet. If you want a fully English card instead, see our guide to English wedding invitation wording.

Frequently asked questions

How do I write a wedding invitation in Hindi?

Start with a blessing, name the hosts, write the couple's names with honorifics such as चि. for the groom and आयु. or सौ.कां. for the bride, add the date, time and venue of each function and close with स्वागतोत्सुक and the family name.

What does चि. mean on a Hindi wedding card?

चि. is short for चिरंजीव, meaning "may you live long". It is traditionally written before the groom's name.

What is a bal manuhar on a shadi card?

It is a short, playful invitation line written as if from the children of the family, asking guests not to miss the wedding. It usually appears at the end of the card.

Can I mix Hindi and English on one wedding card?

Yes. Many families use Hindi for the blessing and family lines and English for event details so every guest can read the timings and venues.
