Punjabi wedding invitation card wording for Anand Karaj and every function

Punjabi weddings are famous for their energy, but the invitation still begins with gratitude. Here is how to word a Punjabi wedding invitation card, with samples in Gurmukhi and English.

A Punjabi wedding invitation card has to carry a lot: the Anand Karaj in the morning, a jaago that goes late into the night, the chooda and the maiyan, and a reception that nobody leaves early. Sikh families usually open with Ik Onkar and invite guests to the gurdwara, while Hindu Punjabi families may open with a Ganesh blessing. Both share the same warmth.

Please note: the Gurmukhi samples below use simple wording. Ask a family elder or a fluent Punjabi speaker to review your final text, and confirm the gurdwara timings with the granthi or management committee.

The structure of a Punjabi wedding card

  1. Opening: "ੴ" (Ik Onkar) or "ੴ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਪ੍ਰਸਾਦਿ" for Sikh families; a Ganesh blessing for many Hindu Punjabi families.
  2. Hosts: the parents, often with grandparents' blessings mentioned.
  3. The couple: names with parents' names.
  4. The main ceremony: Anand Karaj at the gurdwara, or the pheras.
  5. Other functions: roka, chunni, mehndi, jaago, chooda, maiyan, reception.
  6. Closing: a line asking for the guests' blessings, with the family name.

The same invitation in English

The Anand Karaj usually takes place in the morning, and guests are expected to cover their heads in the gurdwara. A gentle note on the card helps first-time guests: "Kindly cover your head inside the Gurdwara Sahib."

Function lines for a Punjabi wedding

Family-only rituals such as the chooda ceremony are often shared only with close relatives. List the events where you expect guests and keep the rest for a family message.

Fun Punjabi lines for friends

Design ideas for Punjabi wedding cards

  • Phulkari embroidery in deep reds, oranges and golds, the most beloved Punjabi motif.
  • Khanda or Ik Onkar in gold for Sikh weddings, placed with respect at the top.
  • Mustard fields and a dawn sky for a softer, rural look.
  • Jaago gaggar (the decorated pot carried at jaago) as a playful illustration.
  • Bright pink, orange and green for a festive, modern card.

Treat religious symbols with care: keep them at the top, never crop or distort them, and avoid placing them on items likely to be thrown away. Many families prefer digital invites partly for this reason.

Roka and chunni invitations

Smaller events like the roka or chunni chadana are usually shared with close family by phone or message rather than on the main card.

These are warm, simple and personal, and they keep the main card focused on the events where everyone is invited.

Frequently asked questions

What is written at the top of a Sikh wedding card?

Most Sikh wedding cards open with ੴ (Ik Onkar), often as "ੴ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਪ੍ਰਸਾਦਿ", followed by the invitation from the family.

What time is the Anand Karaj?

The Anand Karaj is traditionally held in the morning at the gurdwara. Confirm the exact time with the gurdwara and print it clearly on the card.

Which functions go on a Punjabi wedding card?

Commonly the mehndi or sangeet, jaago, Anand Karaj or pheras, and the reception. Family rituals like chooda and maiyan are sometimes listed for close relatives only.

Can I send a Punjabi wedding card on WhatsApp?

Yes. Many families send a digital invitation on WhatsApp alongside a small number of printed cards for elders.
