Kannada wedding invitation and marriage card wording

A Kannada wedding card, the lagna patrike, is graceful and to the point: the blessing, the families, the muhurtha and a warm request to come and bless the couple. Here is how to write one.

Kannada weddings, whether in Bengaluru, Mysuru, Mangaluru or Hubballi, share a few beloved moments: the kashi yatre, when the groom playfully sets off for Kashi until the bride's family persuades him to stay; the dhare, when the bride's parents give her hand; and the muhurtha itself. A Kannada wedding invitation names these moments with respect and asks guests to bless the couple.

Please note: the Kannada samples below use simple, common wording. Customs differ between regions and communities, so please ask an elder or a fluent Kannada speaker to review your final text and confirm the muhurtha with your family priest.

The structure of a Kannada lagna patrike

  1. Invocation: "ಶ್ರೀ ಗಣೇಶಾಯ ನಮಃ" or the family deity (ಕುಲದೇವರು).
  2. The occasion: ಶುಭ ವಿವಾಹ ಆಮಂತ್ರಣ.
  3. The couple: names with honorifics and parents' names.
  4. Muhurtha: date and time.
  5. Venue: the kalyana mantapa and address.
  6. The request to attend with family and bless the couple.
  7. Other events: ನಾಂದಿ, ಕಾಶಿ ಯಾತ್ರೆ, ಆರತಕ್ಷತೆ (reception).
  8. Closing: the family's name with a welcoming line.

Function lines

In many Kannada weddings the reception, or aaratakshate, takes place on the evening before the muhurtha. Say clearly which day each event falls on so guests travelling in do not mix them up.

Wording for friends

Design ideas for a Kannada wedding card

  • Mysuru painting style, with fine gold detailing.
  • Hampi stone chariot or temple pillars for a heritage feel.
  • Mysuru silk colours: deep green, maroon and gold borders.
  • Banana trees and mango leaves, framing the mantapa.
  • Rangoli corners in white on red, a common South Indian motif.

Printed lagna patrike or digital invitation?

Printed cards remain important for elders, often given with betel leaves and areca nuts as part of the formal invitation. Kannada wedding moments explained

  • Naandi: a puja to seek blessings for the wedding, held in both homes.
  • Kashi yatre: the groom pretends to leave for Kashi and is persuaded to marry by the bride's family.
  • Dhare: the bride's parents place her hand in the groom's, with water poured over a coconut and betel leaves.
  • Saptapadi: the seven steps around the sacred fire.

Mentioning one or two of these on your invitation helps guests from outside Karnataka enjoy the ceremony.

Frequently asked questions

How do you write a wedding invitation in Kannada?

Open with "ಶ್ರೀ ಗಣೇಶಾಯ ನಮಃ", write "ಶುಭ ವಿವಾಹ ಆಮಂತ್ರಣ", give the couple's names with honorifics and parents' names, the date, muhurtha and mantapa, and request guests to attend with family and bless the couple.

What is a Kannada wedding card called?

It is commonly called a ಲಗ್ನ ಪತ್ರಿಕೆ (lagna patrike) or ಮದುವೆ ಆಮಂತ್ರಣ ಪತ್ರಿಕೆ (wedding invitation card).

What is aaratakshate?

Aaratakshate is the reception in a Kannada wedding, often held the evening before the muhurtha, where guests meet and bless the couple.

Can I send a Kannada wedding invitation on WhatsApp?

Yes. Digital invitations are common, especially for guests outside Karnataka, alongside printed cards for elders.
