Wedding save the dates: when to send, what to say and picture ideas

A save-the-date is a gentle early heads-up: the date and the city, so guests can plan leave and travel. Here is everything you need for wedding save the dates, including picture ideas and copy-ready wording.

Indian weddings often involve travel: relatives flying in from abroad, friends from other cities and destination weddings in Udaipur, Goa or Jaipur. A save-the-date gives everyone time to plan before the full invitation is ready. It is short, joyful and, increasingly, digital.

When to send a save-the-date

Wedding typeWhen to send
Destination wedding6 to 8 months ahead
Guests travelling from abroad6 months ahead
Wedding in another city3 to 4 months ahead
Local wedding with a long holiday period2 to 3 months ahead

Send it once you have confirmed the date and the city. You do not need final venues or timings yet.

What to include

  • Your names.
  • The date or dates, including multi-day celebrations.
  • The city or venue, if confirmed.
  • "Formal invitation to follow", so guests know more details are coming.
  • Optional: a photo, an Add to calendar button and a contact for travel queries.

Keep it short. A save-the-date that lists every function starts to compete with the invitation.

Save the date picture ideas

A save-the-date is the perfect place for one beautiful image, because there is so little text. Some ideas:

  • A calendar shot: holding a calendar or a board with the date.
  • The ring: a close-up of the engagement ring with the date in the corner.
  • At the destination: a photo at the city or venue where you will marry.
  • Silhouettes at sunrise or sunset, elegant and private.
  • Illustrated art of the city, such as a lake, a palace or a beach, instead of a photo.
  • Childhood photos side by side, with "Then… and now we're getting married".

Keep the date and names on a plain part of the picture so they are easy to read on a phone. For more on choosing photos, see wedding invitations with photos.

Printed or digital save-the-dates?

Printed save-the-dates such as cards or magnets are charming but add cost and posting time, which matters if guests are abroad. Digital save-the-dates arrive instantly, can include an Add to calendar button and cost nothing to send. Most Indian couples now send them on WhatsApp and email.

Save-the-date etiquette

  1. Only send a save-the-date to people who will receive the full invitation.
  2. Be clear about who is invited, especially regarding partners and children.
  3. Follow up with the formal invitation 6 to 8 weeks before the wedding.
  4. If your date changes, tell everyone promptly and personally.

Save the dates for friends and family

For friends, you can be playful, as in our wedding invitation card for friends guide. For elders, keep the tone warm and simple and follow up with a call.

A quick checklist before you send

  1. Confirm the date with both families and, if you can, with the venue or city hotels.
  2. Decide who is invited, and use the same list you will use for the invitation.
  3. Check the spelling of both names and the date format: "14 February 2027" is clearer than "14/02/27" for guests in different countries.
  4. Add a contact for travel questions if many guests are flying in.
  5. Send a test to yourself and open it on a phone to check the text is readable.
  6. Note who you have sent it to, so nobody is missed when the invitation goes out.

If you are inviting guests from abroad, mention the nearest airport and any visa or travel tips in a follow-up message rather than on the save-the-date itself. Keep the save-the-date simple and joyful; practical details can come later.

Frequently asked questions

How early should you send a wedding save-the-date?

Six to eight months ahead for destination weddings or guests abroad, and three to four months ahead for weddings in another city.

What should a save-the-date say?

Your names, the date, the city or venue and "formal invitation to follow". Keep it short; full details belong in the invitation.

Do I need a photo on my save-the-date?

No, but a save the date picture makes it more personal. Illustrations of your wedding city are a lovely alternative.

Can I send a save-the-date on WhatsApp?

Yes. Digital save-the-dates are common and convenient, especially for guests who need to book travel.
