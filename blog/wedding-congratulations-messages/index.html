Wedding congratulations messages for every couple

"Congratulations" is the one word everyone uses, which is exactly why it needs a little help. These wedding congratulations messages are sorted by relationship and setting, so you can find one that fits in seconds.

A congratulations message is slightly different from a blessing or a wish. It recognises something the couple have done: found each other, planned a wedding, brought two families together. That makes it the natural reply when you receive a wedding invitation, see the engagement announcement or read the news on a work email.

Where marriage congratulations messages are used

  • Replying to an invitation, before you have even confirmed your RSVP.
  • Inside a gift envelope or card at the reception.
  • On a work thread when a colleague announces their wedding.
  • Under a social media post with the wedding photos.
  • In a letter to a client or business contact, where tone matters most.

Missing a wedding is common when families are spread across cities and countries. A thoughtful message makes up for a lot.

How to make congratulations sound sincere

Sincerity comes from specifics. Mention how they met, a quality you admire in the couple or something from the invitation itself. A line like "The invite with the palace doors was so you" shows you actually opened it, and couples notice.

Avoid starting with "Finally!" or making jokes about how long it took. Even with close friends, it can land badly when parents are reading over their shoulder.

Reply to a digital wedding invitation

Many couples now send a wedding invitation website instead of a printed card. These usually have a WhatsApp RSVP button that opens a pre-filled message. Add a line of congratulations before you hit send and the couple get your RSVP and your wishes in one place, which makes their guest list easier too.

Frequently asked questions

How do you say congratulations on a wedding formally?

"Please accept our warmest congratulations on your wedding. We wish you both a lifetime of happiness." This works for clients, seniors and anyone you address formally.

Is it "congratulations on your wedding" or "congratulations for your wedding"?

"Congratulations on your wedding" is the standard English phrase. "Congratulations for" is commonly heard in India but "on" sounds more natural in writing.

What do you write in a wedding card if you do not know the couple well?

Keep it simple: "Congratulations on your wedding. Wishing you both every happiness for the years ahead." Then sign your name and, if useful, how you know the family.

Can I send marriage congratulations on email?

Yes, especially for colleagues and business contacts. Use a clear subject line such as "Congratulations on your wedding" and keep the message to a few warm sentences.
