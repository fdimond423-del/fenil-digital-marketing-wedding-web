Christian wedding invitation card wording, with Bible verses

A Christian wedding card invites guests to witness a sacred covenant, usually in church, followed by a joyful reception. Here is wording for Christian wedding invitations, with verses and samples you can adapt.

Christian weddings in India are as varied as the communities that celebrate them: Goan and Mangalorean Catholics with their Roce the day before, Syrian Christians in Kerala, Protestant churches in the North-East, Tamil Nadu and beyond. What unites them is the church ceremony, a reading from Scripture and a celebration with family and friends. A Christian marriage invitation card reflects this with reverent wording and, very often, a Bible verse.

The structure of a Christian wedding invitation

  1. A verse or blessing at the top, such as "By the grace of God".
  2. Hosts: the parents, or the couple with their families.
  3. The request line: "request the honour of your presence" for a church ceremony.
  4. The occasion: Holy Matrimony, the Sacrament of Marriage or a Nuptial Mass.
  5. The church, date and time.
  6. The reception, usually on the same evening.
  7. RSVP details.

Roce and pre-wedding lines

Goan and Mangalorean Catholic families celebrate the Roce, when the bride or groom is anointed with coconut milk and oil by family and friends. It is often a more casual event.

Bible verses for wedding invitations

Choose a verse that speaks to you as a couple and quote it exactly from your preferred translation. A few popular choices:

  • 1 Corinthians 13:4–7: "Love is patient, love is kind…", the most loved wedding passage.
  • Ruth 1:16: "Where you go I will go, and where you stay I will stay."
  • Ecclesiastes 4:12: "A cord of three strands is not quickly broken."
  • Genesis 2:24: about two becoming one flesh.
  • Song of Songs 8:7: "Many waters cannot quench love."
  • Colossians 3:14: "And over all these virtues put on love, which binds them all together in perfect unity."
  • 1 John 4:19: "We love because he first loved us."

Wording differs slightly between translations, so copy the verse from the Bible your church uses and include the reference.

Design ideas for Christian wedding cards

  • Church facades, from whitewashed Goan chapels to Gothic cathedrals.
  • Lilies, white roses and olive branches, classic symbols of purity and peace.
  • A simple cross or two interlinked rings, placed respectfully.
  • Doves, symbolising the Holy Spirit and peace.
  • Ivory, white, sage and gold for an elegant palette; teal and coral for a Goan touch.

Practical tips

  1. Confirm the church timings with the parish office before printing. Many churches have strict start times.
  2. Mention whether the reception is at a different venue and how far it is.
  3. If guests from other faiths are invited, a small note about church etiquette can help, such as arriving before the bride and dressing modestly.
  4. For a long gap between the church and the reception, suggest a place for guests to rest.

Frequently asked questions

What is written on a Christian wedding invitation card?

A verse or blessing, the hosts, the request line, the couple's names and parents, the church with date and time, the reception details and RSVP information.

Which Bible verse is best for a wedding invitation?

1 Corinthians 13:4–7 is the most popular. Ruth 1:16, Ecclesiastes 4:12 and Colossians 3:14 are also widely used. Choose one that is meaningful to you both and quote it from your church's translation.

Is it "Holy Matrimony" or "Nuptial Mass"?

"Holy Matrimony" refers to the marriage itself and is used across denominations. "Nuptial Mass" is used in Catholic weddings when the marriage takes place within a Mass.

What is a Roce?

The Roce is a Goan and Mangalorean Catholic pre-wedding ceremony where family and friends anoint the bride or groom with coconut milk and oil, usually held the day before the wedding.
