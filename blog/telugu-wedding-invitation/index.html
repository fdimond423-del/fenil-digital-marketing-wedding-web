Telugu wedding invitation wording for your subhalekha

In Telugu homes the wedding card is the subhalekha, the auspicious letter. Here is how it is structured, with simple sample wording in Telugu and English.

A Telugu wedding moves towards one sacred moment: the muhurtham, when the couple place jeelakarra bellam, a paste of cumin and jaggery, on each other's heads. The subhalekha announces that moment with care, names both families with honour and invites guests to come with their entire family.

Please note: the Telugu samples below use simple, common wording. Families in Andhra Pradesh, Telangana and elsewhere follow their own customs, so please ask an elder or a fluent Telugu speaker to review your final text and confirm the muhurtham with your purohit.

The structure of a Telugu subhalekha

  1. Invocation: "శ్రీ గణేశాయ నమః" or a family deity such as Sri Venkateswara.
  2. The occasion: శుభలేఖ or వివాహ మహోత్సవ ఆహ్వానం.
  3. The couple: with honorifics and parents' names.
  4. Muhurtham: the date and the exact time.
  5. Venue: the kalyana mandapam or function hall.
  6. The request to attend with family and bless the couple.
  7. Other events: పెళ్ళికూతురు, పెళ్ళికొడుకు, reception.
  8. Closing: the host family's name.

Function lines

Many Telugu weddings take place at night or very early in the morning, depending on the muhurtham. Write the time clearly and, if it falls late at night, add a line about dinner timings.

Wording for friends

Design ideas for a Telugu wedding card

  • Kalyana mandapam illustrations with banana trees and mango-leaf thoranams.
  • Lord Venkateswara or Ganesha at the top, depicted respectfully.
  • Kalamkari art, the hand-painted textile style from Andhra Pradesh, for borders and motifs.
  • Turmeric yellow, kumkum red and gold for a bright, traditional palette.
  • Pochampally ikat patterns for a Telangana-inspired modern look.

Printed subhalekha or digital invite?

The first subhalekha is often placed before the family deity, and printed cards are still taken to elders with turmeric and kumkum on the corners. Telugu wedding moments guests love

  • Kashi yatra: the groom playfully sets off for Kashi until the bride's brother persuades him to stay.
  • Jeelakarra bellam: the couple place cumin and jaggery paste on each other's heads at the muhurtham.
  • Talambralu: the couple shower each other with rice mixed with turmeric, often with much laughter.
  • Arundhati nakshatram: the couple are shown the Arundhati star, a symbol of devotion.

A short line about one of these moments on your invitation or website helps guests from other traditions know what to watch for.

Frequently asked questions

What is a Telugu wedding card called?

It is called a శుభలేఖ (subhalekha), meaning an auspicious letter.

What does చి.ల.సౌ. mean on a Telugu wedding card?

It is short for చిరంజీవి లక్ష్మీ సౌభాగ్యవతి, a traditional blessing used before the bride's name.

What should a Telugu wedding invitation include?

A blessing, the couple's names with honorifics and their parents' names, the date and muhurtham time, the venue, a request to attend with family, and the details of other functions.

Can I send a Telugu wedding invitation on WhatsApp?

Yes. Digital subhalekhas are common now, especially for guests in other cities and abroad.
