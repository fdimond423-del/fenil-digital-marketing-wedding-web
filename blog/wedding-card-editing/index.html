Wedding card editing: how to edit text, photos and fonts without mistakes

Most wedding card problems are editing problems: a misspelt name, a wrong weekday, a broken Hindi font. This guide shows you how to edit a wedding card carefully, whether it is a printed design, an image or a digital invite.

Wedding card editing usually happens late at night, with five relatives sending corrections on WhatsApp. That is exactly when mistakes slip through. A calm, systematic approach saves you from reprints, awkward phone calls and a card that tells guests to arrive on the wrong day.

What you might need to edit

  • Names: spellings, honorifics and the order of family members.
  • Dates and times: including the weekday and the muhurat.
  • Venues: names, addresses and map links.
  • Wording: blessings, request lines and closing lines.
  • Photos: crops, brightness and replacing images.
  • Design: colours, fonts and layout.

Tools people use

Couples edit wedding cards in many ways: online design tools such as Canva, desktop software such as Photoshop or Illustrator, presentation software, mobile editing apps, or by sending corrections to a designer or printer. Whatever you use, the same principles apply. See our guide to Canva wedding invitations if you are designing yourself.

Editing text the right way

  1. Collect all corrections in one place. Ask each side of the family to send their changes to one person in one message.
  2. Edit from a master document. Keep the final wording in a single text file and copy from it into the design.
  3. Change one thing at a time and tick it off.
  4. Never retype names from memory. Copy them from the master document.
  5. Check the weekday against the date using a calendar for your wedding year.

Fixing Hindi and regional script problems

Editing text in Devanagari, Gurmukhi, Bengali, Gujarati, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada or Urdu can go wrong in design software:

  • Broken conjuncts and matras: letters that appear separately instead of joining. This usually means the font or software does not support the script properly. Switch to a well-known Unicode font for that script.
  • Boxes or question marks: the font does not include those characters.
  • Wrong direction for Urdu: Urdu and Arabic must flow right to left. Check every line.
  • Copy-paste errors from old PDFs: retype from the master document instead.

Always ask a fluent reader to check the final version on a phone, not just on the design screen.

Editing photos

  • Crop to faces. On small cards and phones, wide shots lose detail.
  • Brighten gently. Lift shadows on faces without washing out outfits.
  • Keep colours natural. Strong filters can turn red into orange and gold into yellow.
  • Use original files, not images forwarded on WhatsApp.
  • Remove distractions carefully, such as a stray bag in the background, without over-editing.

The final proofreading checklist

CheckDone?
Every name spelt correctly, verified by that family
Honorifics consistent (Mr/Mrs, Shri/Smt, चि./सौ.कां.)
Day, date and time correct for each function
Weekday matches the date in your wedding year
Venue names and addresses correct
Map links open the right place
RSVP contact number tested
Regional script checked by a fluent reader
Text readable on a phone screen
No text too close to the edges for printing

Print it out or tick it off on your phone. Two different people should go through it independently.

Editing after the card is sent

With printed cards, an error after printing means a reprint, a correction slip or a lot of phone calls. With images on WhatsApp, you need to send a corrected image and hope everyone sees the new one. With a wedding invitation website, you update the page and everyone with the link sees the corrected version, which is one reason many couples choose a website for the details most likely to change, such as timings and venues.

Editing WhatsApp image cards

If your invitation is an image shared on WhatsApp, export it at a size that stays sharp on phones without being too heavy to download, and keep the text large. Name each version clearly, such as "invite-final-v3", so you never send an old file by mistake. When you send a corrected image, say clearly what changed, for example "Updated: reception now starts at 8 pm".

Frequently asked questions

How can I edit a wedding card online?

You can use an online design tool, a mobile editing app or ask your designer or printer to make the changes. Keep a master text file of the final wording and copy from it to avoid errors.

Why does my Hindi text look broken in my wedding card?

The font or the software may not fully support Devanagari, so conjuncts and matras do not join correctly. Use a well-known Unicode Devanagari font and check the result on a phone.

Can I change a wedding invitation after sending it?

With a printed card or image you need to send a correction. With a wedding invitation website, you can update the page and everyone sees the new details on the same link.

Who should proofread a wedding card?

At least two people: someone from each family to check names, and a fluent reader for any regional language text.
