Whether you are writing in a card, on WhatsApp or in the comments under the wedding photo, here are fresh wedding greetings in English for every relationship and tone.

Indian weddings are long, loud and full of people, so the wedding day wishes you send often get read in a rush between the haldi and the pheras. A good marriage greeting is short enough to read in a moment and warm enough to be remembered later. All of the messages below are written in simple English, so they work for relatives who prefer English and for mixed-language families.

What to say when wishing someone on their wedding day

There is no single right formula, but most happy wedding greetings combine a blessing with something about the couple. Here are a few simple rules that keep your message from sounding copied.

  • Address both people. Even if you only know the bride or the groom, wish them as a couple.
  • Keep the day in focus. "Today" and "this new journey" feel more present than generic lines about love.
  • Choose one tone. Mixing a joke with a solemn blessing usually reads awkwardly.
  • Sign off clearly. In a family WhatsApp group, add your name and how you are related.

Writing marriage wishes in a card versus a message

In a printed card you can write three or four lines and sign with your family's name. On WhatsApp, two lines plus an emoji is plenty, because the couple may be reading hundreds of messages at once. If you are writing a speech or toast, pick one of the heartfelt greetings above and expand it with a story.

When the wedding invite itself is a link, such as a wedding invitation website, many guests reply to the RSVP message with their wishes. That is a lovely moment to use one of the short lines above.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best short wedding greeting in English?

"Wishing you both a lifetime of love and happiness. Happy wedding day!" It is short, warm and suitable for anyone from a colleague to a cousin.

What do you say when wishing someone a happy marriage?

Wish them love, understanding and happiness together, and if you can, mention something you admire about them as a couple. A simple "Happy married life" is also widely used in India.

Should I wish the couple before or after the wedding?

Both are fine. Many people send wishes on the morning of the wedding and congratulate the couple again once the ceremony is over or when they see the photos.

Is "happy marriage day" correct English?

People will understand it, but "Happy wedding day" for the day itself and "Happy married life" afterwards sound more natural in English.
