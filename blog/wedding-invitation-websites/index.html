Wedding invitation websites: how a website wedding invitation works

A website wedding invitation is a single link that holds every event, map, photo and RSVP. Here is how they work, what to look for in wedding invitation sites and when one makes sense for an Indian wedding.

Indian weddings have a scheduling problem. Four or five functions, three venues, relatives flying in from different cities and a WhatsApp group that buries the important details under good-morning messages. A wedding invitation website solves this by putting everything behind one link that guests can open again and again.

What is a wedding invitation website?

It is a web page, usually designed for phones, that works as your invitation. Instead of a fixed image or a printed card, guests open a link and see your names, every function with its date, time and venue, maps, photos and a way to RSVP. Because it is a live page, you can update a timing or a venue and everyone sees the change on the same link.

It is different from a big wedding planning website with registries and guest accounts. An invitation website is focused on one job: inviting guests beautifully and getting them to the right place on time.

What a good invitation website includes

  • Your names and a blessing, presented beautifully at the top.
  • Every function with day, date, time, venue and dress code.
  • A map button for each venue, so guests can navigate directly.
  • RSVP in a format your guests actually use. In India, that usually means WhatsApp.
  • A photo gallery of your pre-wedding shoot or favourite pictures.
  • A countdown to the big day.
  • Optional music that plays only when guests tap it.
  • Practical notes: parking, stay, weather and etiquette.

What to look for in wedding invitation sites

There are many wedding invitation sites and apps. Before choosing one, check:

  1. Phone-first design: most guests will open it on a phone, inside WhatsApp.
  2. Speed: it should load quickly on mobile data, even at a crowded venue.
  3. No app or login for guests: elders should be able to open it with one tap.
  4. Indian functions: haldi, mehendi, sangeet, Nikah, Anand Karaj, Muhurtham, Roce and reception, not just "ceremony" and "party".
  5. WhatsApp RSVP and maps, built in.
  6. How long it stays online after the wedding.
  7. Who can see it: whether the page is hidden from search engines and only reachable by link.
  8. Total cost: one-time or subscription, and what is included.

Privacy and safety

An invitation website shows names, dates and venues, so be thoughtful:

  • Share the link privately in chats rather than posting it publicly.
  • Avoid adding home addresses for family-only events; share those separately.
  • Choose photos you are comfortable with anyone holding the link seeing.
  • Ask whether the page can be kept out of search results.

How to share it on WhatsApp

  1. Send the link in a personal message with a short note, for example: "We're getting married! Here's our invite with all the details."
  2. For family groups, pin the message so it stays at the top.
  3. Before each function, send the link again with "See you tonight! Tap 'Get directions' for the venue."
  4. For elders, call to tell them a link is coming and how to open it.

Frequently asked questions

What is a website wedding invitation?

It is a wedding invitation in the form of a web page, opened through a link. It holds every function, venue map, photos and an RSVP option, and it can be updated after sending.

Are wedding invitation websites suitable for elders?

Yes, if the design is simple and readable and opens without any app or login. A phone call to elders explaining that a link is coming helps a lot.

How long do wedding invitation websites stay online?

It depends on the provider. Fenil Digital Marketing invitations stay online until 12 months after your wedding date.

Can I change details after sending the invitation link?

Yes. Updating the page changes it for everyone who has the link, which is one of the biggest advantages over printed cards.
