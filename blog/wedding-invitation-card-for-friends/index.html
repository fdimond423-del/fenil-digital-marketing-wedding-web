Wedding invitation card for friends: wording that sounds like you

Your parents' invitation can be formal. The one you send your friends should sound like the group chat. Here is wording for a wedding invitation card for friends, plus tips on what to include so nobody misses the sangeet.

In many Indian weddings there are really two guest lists. The family list follows tradition: the parents' names, the blessings, the formal "cordially invite". The friends' list is where you can relax, joke a little and highlight the events your friends actually care about, usually the haldi, the sangeet and the after-party. A separate friends invitation card for marriage lets you speak to both groups in the right voice.

What a friends' wedding invitation should include

Friends need practical details more than ceremony. Make sure your card or message covers:

  • Your names, clearly. Friends may know you by a nickname, but the partner's friends may not.
  • Which events they are invited to. Be clear if the group is invited to the sangeet and reception but not the pheras, or the other way round.
  • Dates, times and venues, with a map link for each venue.
  • Dress code or theme for each event, because this is the question you will be asked most.
  • Stay and travel details if people are coming from out of town.
  • How to RSVP, and by when.

A wedding invitation website is especially useful for friends because you send one link in the group, and everyone gets the map for each venue, the dress codes and a WhatsApp RSVP button. You can also share a separate, more traditional version with the family list.

Tips for inviting friends without hurt feelings

  1. Decide early whether partners and plus-ones are invited, and say so politely in the card.
  2. If you cannot invite a whole office or group, invite people personally rather than in a public group.
  3. Follow up digital invites with a phone call for the friends who matter most.
  4. Send friends a reminder two days before, with the venue map again.

Frequently asked questions

What do you write in a wedding invitation card for friends?

Your names, the events they are invited to, dates, times and venues, the dress code and how to RSVP, written in a warm, informal tone. The casual samples above are ready to adapt.

Is it okay to send a separate invitation to friends?

Yes, it is common in India. Families often send a formal card to relatives and elders while the couple send a more casual invitation to their friends.

How early should I invite friends to my wedding?

Send a save-the-date three to six months ahead for destination weddings, and the full invitation six to eight weeks before the first event.

Can I invite friends on WhatsApp only?

Yes, especially for friends you message regularly. A link to a wedding invitation website works well in WhatsApp groups because the details stay in one place.
