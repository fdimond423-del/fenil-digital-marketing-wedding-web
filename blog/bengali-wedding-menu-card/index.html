Bengali wedding menu card ideas, with menu wording in Bengali and English

At a Bengali wedding, guests remember the food long after the photos. A good menu card lists the courses in the order they arrive, so nobody fills up on luchi before the kosha mangsho.

The Bengali wedding feast, served at the biye and again at the bou bhaat, follows a beloved sequence: something fried to start, a few vegetarian dishes, then fish, then meat, then chutney and finally sweets. The menu card is part of the fun. Guests read it at the table, plan their appetite and often take it home.

Please note: Bengali spellings of dishes vary between families and caterers. The names below use common spellings; ask your caterer and a fluent reader to check the final card.

What a Bengali wedding menu card includes

  • A heading such as "প্রীতিভোজ" (wedding feast) or "আজকের মেনু" (today's menu).
  • The occasion and names, for example "অনন্যা ও রোহনের শুভ বিবাহ উপলক্ষে".
  • Courses in serving order, often grouped.
  • Optional notes: vegetarian alternatives, a thank-you line or the date.

You do not need all of these. Many families choose one dish from each group, and the menu for the bou bhaat is often a little different from the biye so guests who attend both get variety.

Menu card wording in Bengali

Menu card wording in English

A bilingual card, with Bengali on one side and English on the other, works well when you have guests from outside Bengal. They get to learn what they are eating, and the elders still see the familiar names.

Vegetarian and special menus

If you have vegetarian guests, add a short line such as "নিরামিষ পদ অনুরোধে পাওয়া যাবে" (vegetarian dishes available on request) or list a separate vegetarian column: paneer or chhanar dalna, potol dorma and a vegetable chop. Mention any dish with nuts or prawns clearly for guests with allergies.

Design ideas for a Bengali menu card

  • Banana leaf green borders, echoing how the feast was traditionally served.
  • Alpana corners in white on red or maroon.
  • Illustrated fish motifs, a symbol of prosperity at Bengali weddings.
  • Terracotta tones inspired by Bishnupur temples.
  • Tall, narrow cards that stand on the table or sit on each plate.

Printing the menu card

Menu cards are small and need to be printed close to the date, once the caterer finalises dishes. Ask your caterer whether they include menu cards; many do. Frequently asked questions

What is served at a Bengali wedding?

Typically fried starters like fish fry, luchi or radhaballavi, pulao, dal and vegetables, fish such as bhetki or prawn, mutton, chutney, papad and sweets like rosogolla, sandesh and mishti doi, followed by paan.

What is written at the top of a Bengali menu card?

Common headings are "প্রীতিভোজ" (wedding feast) or "আজকের মেনু" (today's menu), followed by the couple's names and the occasion.

Should a Bengali wedding menu card be in Bengali or English?

Either works. A bilingual card with Bengali and English names is a thoughtful choice when you have guests from outside Bengal.

Is the bou bhaat menu different from the wedding menu?

Often, yes. Families usually vary a few dishes so guests attending both meals enjoy something new.
