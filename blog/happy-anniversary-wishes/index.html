Wishing someone a happy anniversary is easy to put off and easy to get wrong. A forwarded image with glitter text does the job, but a line that remembers them lands far better. Below you will find wedding anniversary greetings sorted by who you are writing to, followed by a short guide on how to make any of them sound like you.

How to write an anniversary wish that feels personal

The best anniversary wishes have three small parts: the occasion, one specific memory or quality, and a hope for the year ahead. You do not need all three every time, but even one specific detail turns a generic greeting into something people save.

  • Name the milestone. "Ten years" or "your first anniversary" tells them you actually know the date.
  • Add one detail. The road trip where the car broke down, the way they still share chai every morning, the dance at the sangeet.
  • End with a wish, not a lecture. Keep advice for the toast. A wish should feel light.
  • Match the channel. A WhatsApp message can be two lines. A handwritten card can take a paragraph.

Frequently asked questions

What is a simple way of wishing a happy anniversary?

"Happy anniversary to you both. Wishing you many more years of love and laughter together." It works for almost anyone, and you can add a name or a memory to make it personal.

How do you wish a happy wedding anniversary to elders respectfully?

Keep it short and warm: wish them health, happiness and togetherness, and avoid jokes unless you know they enjoy them. A phone call or a voice note after your message is always appreciated.

Is it okay to send anniversary greetings on WhatsApp?

Yes. For most people a thoughtful WhatsApp message is perfectly normal. For parents or a milestone year, consider a call, a handwritten card or joining the celebration as well.

What do you write in a 25th anniversary card?

Mention the milestone, thank them for what their marriage has meant to the family, and wish them good health and many more years together. The silver anniversary line above is a good starting point.
