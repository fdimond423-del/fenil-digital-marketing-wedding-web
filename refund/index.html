Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated 18 November 2026.

Before we start

If you cancel before you have sent us your details, we refund the full amount.

If we cannot deliver

If we cannot deliver your invite, you get a full refund.

After delivery

Because your invite is a personalised digital product, we cannot refund it once your invite has been delivered. Tell us what is wrong and we will fix it within the included changes.

Done-for-you setup

The done-for-you setup add-on is refundable until we begin entering your content.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay, usually within 5–7 working days after we approve them, depending on your bank.

How to ask
